01Who we are

Acme Interiors Private Limited is responsible for the personal data collected through this website. You can contact us about privacy at privacy@your-domain.com.

02What we collect

  • Enquiries: your name, company, work email, phone number and details about your project
  • Job applications: your name, contact details, CV, portfolio link and anything else you choose to send
  • Website usage: basic technical information such as browser type, device and pages visited, if analytics are enabled

03How we use it

  • To respond to enquiries and prepare proposals
  • To assess job applications and contact candidates
  • To deliver and manage projects you engage us for
  • To improve this website and our services
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations

We do not sell your personal data.

04Legal basis and consent

We process personal data with your consent (for example, when you submit a form) or where it is needed to take steps toward, or perform, a contract with you. You can withdraw consent at any time by contacting us.

05Sharing your data

We share personal data only with people who need it to help us operate, such as hosting providers, email and CRM tools, and, for live projects, consultants and contractors working on your project. They may use it only for those purposes.

06How long we keep it

We keep enquiry data for up to 24 months after our last contact, and job applications for up to 12 months unless you ask us to delete them sooner. Project records are kept as long as the law requires.

07Cookies

This website does / does not use cookies or analytics. If it does, we use them only to understand how the site is used. You can block cookies in your browser settings.

08Your rights

Under applicable data protection law, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct or update inaccurate data
  • Have your data erased
  • Withdraw consent you have given
  • Raise a grievance about how we handled your data

To make a request, email privacy@your-domain.com. We will respond within 30 days.

09Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against loss, misuse and unauthorised access. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

10Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed.
